# Founder-led sales

The primary go-to-market motion until repeatable ICP, use case, channel,
and onboarding are identified (`AGENTS.md` §63–65). The founder should be
close to every early customer conversation — this is deliberate, not a
temporary staffing gap to fix quickly.

## Daily workflow

1. **Research** — add or refine target accounts
   (`docs/sales/target-account-template.md`).
2. **Personalize** — never send a template unedited
   (`docs/sales/outreach.md`).
3. **Contact** — send outreach, log it in the pipeline.
4. **Follow up** — work the sequence for everyone mid-pipeline; don't let
   a live thread go cold because something new came in.
5. **Demo** — run `docs/sales/demo-script.md` against real product, not
   slides.
6. **Onboard** — get anyone who signs up to first value (agent connected,
   first event, first policy, first evaluation) fast; watch where they
   actually get stuck.
7. **Interview** — `docs/customer-research/interview.md` with anyone
   willing, converted or not.
8. **Improve** — feed real feedback into
   `docs/customer-research/feature-prioritization.md`; most days this
   should not produce a new feature.

## Objectives, in order

1. **First 10 customers.** Not 100,000 users. The first 10 exist to teach
   why they bought, why others didn't, what they actually connect and use
   daily, and what they'd consider mission-critical (`AGENTS.md` §64).
2. **After the first 10**, look for what repeats: ICP, use case,
   acquisition channel, onboarding path. Don't assume repeatability from
   fewer than ~10 data points.
3. **Then scale toward 100**, using whatever pattern actually repeated —
   not the pattern that was originally guessed at.

## What "founder-led" means in practice

- The founder personally sends the first outreach messages and runs the
  first demos — not a hired SDR reading a script for an unproven pitch.
- Every "no" gets a real reason logged, not just a pipeline status change
  to `LOST`.
- Pricing, positioning, and ICP are all still hypotheses at this stage —
  treat objections and "not right now"s as data, not as noise to push
  past.

## Anti-patterns to avoid

- Optimizing for reply rate or meeting count instead of qualified pipeline
  and real signal (`AGENTS.md` §54).
- Automating outreach at volume before the message is proven to work on
  10 real accounts.
- Building a second big feature roadmap instead of doing this loop — see
  `AGENTS.md` §85's closing principle.
